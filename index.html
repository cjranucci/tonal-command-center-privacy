YOUR FITNESS DATA, EXPLAINED

Privacy policy.

A clear account of what Tonal Command Center does with personal fitness information.

Effective date: November 9, 2026

The essentials

Fitness data is used for personal analytics, not advertising. The dashboard is privately hosted. This public website contains the policy only.

01

Application overview

Tonal Command Center is an independently operated personal fitness analytics dashboard designed to combine Tonal workout data with WHOOP recovery, sleep, activity, and physiological metrics. Its purpose is to help its operator understand training consistency, strength progression, and relationships between training and recovery.

Current integration status: the dashboard currently processes Tonal data. WHOOP integration is planned and is not active as of this policy’s effective date. No WHOOP data or WHOOP authorization tokens are currently collected by this application. The WHOOP descriptions below apply when that integration is enabled and the user grants authorization.

This is a personal project, not an official WHOOP or Tonal service.

02

Information collected from WHOOP

When connected, the application may receive the following information through WHOOP’s API, limited by the permissions the user approves and the information WHOOP makes available:

  • Recovery and physiological metrics: recovery scores, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, and other available recovery-related measurements.
  • Sleep: sleep timing, duration, stages, performance, and available sleep-related physiological measurements.
  • Activity and workouts: activity type, timing and duration, strain, heart-rate summaries or zones, and energy-expenditure estimates where available.
  • Cycles and supporting information: daily cycle summaries, timestamps, time-zone information, and identifiers needed to associate records with the authorized account.

WHOOP access and refresh tokens may be required to maintain an authorized connection. The planned connection uses WHOOP authorization; the application does not ask for a WHOOP account password. Broader profile or body-measurement access is not represented as currently implemented and would require appropriate authorization and disclosure if added.

The dashboard also processes Tonal workout history and optional measurements entered manually by its operator. Those sources are separate from WHOOP.

03

Purpose of data processing

WHOOP data is intended to support personal fitness analytics: displaying recovery and sleep trends, reviewing activity and training load, and comparing those observations with Tonal workouts and strength progression. Supporting identifiers and authorization tokens are used to retrieve and associate authorized records.

WHOOP data is not sold and is not used for advertising, ad targeting, or building advertising profiles. Comparisons between metrics are used to explore trends; they do not establish that one measurement caused another.

04

Local storage and security

The existing dashboard stores fitness history in a local SQLite database on the operator’s computer. Raw exports, operational records, and database backups can also be stored locally. The planned WHOOP integration is intended to use this local storage model.

The current application uses operating-system file permissions, macOS Keychain for its existing Tonal credentials, private-network access controls, and authorization checks on sensitive dashboard actions. WHOOP token storage is not yet implemented; this policy will be updated to reflect its actual handling before that integration is activated.

The application does not itself encrypt its database, exports, or backups at rest. Any device-level disk encryption depends on the operator’s computer configuration and is not guaranteed by this policy. Local access controls and private-network restrictions do not eliminate every security risk.

The public privacy-policy site is separate from the dashboard. It does not connect to the fitness database, offer an account sign-in, or collect workout or WHOOP records.

05

Data sharing and third parties

The application does not publish fitness records or intentionally share them with advertisers, data brokers, or public repositories. No advertising or third-party analytics scripts are included in this privacy-policy website.

  • WHOOP and Tonal: these services provide the underlying fitness records and handle their own accounts and services under their respective policies.
  • Private connectivity: the dashboard uses Tailscale for private network access. That provider handles its own service and network metadata under its policies.
  • Public policy hosting: GitHub Pages hosts this policy. GitHub may process visitor information such as IP addresses and request logs under its own policies. Fitness records and authentication credentials are not included in the public policy repository.
  • Email: if you contact the operator, your email address and message are processed by the email services involved so the operator can respond.

These third-party services operate separately from this application. See WHOOP’s privacy policy, Tailscale’s privacy policy, and GitHub’s privacy statement for their practices.

06

Data retention and deletion

Fitness history is retained locally for longitudinal analysis until the operator removes it. The application does not automatically delete all personal data after a fixed period.

The current housekeeping process retains database backups from the most recent 30 days and at least 14 recent backup snapshots, and export runs from the most recent seven days and at least three recent runs. These minimum snapshot counts can retain some copies longer. Source records in the database, manually copied files, and offline backups may remain until separately removed.

Deleting data from one place does not automatically erase its other copies. Complete removal may require deleting or updating the local database, raw source records, exports, retained backups, and any separately saved copies. There is no currently implemented one-click WHOOP deletion feature or guaranteed automatic deletion schedule.

Contact the operator using the address below to request assistance with access or deletion. Revoking authorization is a separate step and does not itself remove locally retained information.

07

Revoking WHOOP authorization

When a WHOOP connection is enabled, you can revoke its authorization through WHOOP’s connected-app or integration controls, where available. You may also contact the operator for assistance disconnecting the application.

Revocation prevents the application from obtaining further WHOOP data using the revoked authorization. It does not automatically delete records already imported or retained in backups. Request local deletion separately if you also want those copies removed.

WHOOP’s authorization and revocation mechanisms are described in its official OAuth documentation.

08

Changes to this privacy policy

This policy may be updated when integrations or data-handling practices change. The revised policy and its updated effective date will be published at this same URL. In particular, the actual WHOOP integration and token-storage practices will be documented before collection begins.
